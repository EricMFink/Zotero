 "CITY DISPOSAL SYSTEMS, INC" [Page 1](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/PT2BMCUN?page=1&annotation=highlight-p1x236y678)




 "James Brown, a truckdriver employed by respondent, was discharged when he refused to drive a truck that he honestly and reasonably believed to be unsafe because of faulty brakes. the collective-bargaining agreement between respondent and Local 247 of the International Brotherhood of Teamsters," [Page 1](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/PT2BMCUN?page=1&annotation=highlight-p1x70y386)




 ""The Employer shall not require employees to take out on the streets or highways any vehicle that is not in safe operating condition or equipped with the safety appliances *825 prescribed by law. It shall not be a violation of this Agreement where employees refuse to operate such equipment unless such refusal is unjustified."[1]" [Page 1](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/PT2BMCUN?page=1&annotation=highlight-p1x100y307)




 "The question to be decided is whether Brown's honest and reasonable assertion of his right to be free of the obligation to drive unsafe trucks constituted "concerted activit[y]" within the meaning of § 7" [Page 1](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/PT2BMCUN?page=1&annotation=highlight-p1x70y271)




 "The NLRB's decision in this case applied the Board's longstanding "Interboro doctrine," under which an individual's assertion of a right grounded in a collectivebargaining agreement is recognized as "concerted activit[y]" and therefore accorded the protection of § 7" [Page 2](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/PT2BMCUN?page=2&annotation=highlight-p2x70y31)




 "The Board has relied on two justifications for the doctrine: First, the assertion of a right contained in a collective-bargaining agreement is an extension of the concerted action that produced the agreement," [Page 3](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/PT2BMCUN?page=3&annotation=highlight-p3x70y724)




 "and second, the assertion of such a right affects the rights of all employees covered by the collective-bargaining agreement." [Page 3](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/PT2BMCUN?page=3&annotation=highlight-p3x70y697)




 "the task of defining the scope of § 7 "is for the Board to perform in the first instance as it considers the wide variety of cases that come before it" [Page 3](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/PT2BMCUN?page=3&annotation=highlight-p3x70y661)




 "an employee's invocation of a right derived from a collective-bargaining agreement meets § 7's requirement that an employee's action be taken "for purposes of collective bargaining or other mutual aid or protection." a single employee's invocation of such rights affects all the employees that are covered by the collective-bargaining agreement." [Page 3](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/PT2BMCUN?page=3&annotation=highlight-p3x70y503)




 "This type of generalized effect, as our cases have demonstrated, is sufficient to bring the actions of an individual employee within the "mutual aid or protection" standard, regardless of whether the employee has his own interests most immediately in mind." [Page 3](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/PT2BMCUN?page=3&annotation=highlight-p3x70y463)




 "The term "concerted activit[y]" is not defined in the Act but it clearly enough embraces the activities of employees who have joined together in order to achieve common goals" [Page 3](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/PT2BMCUN?page=3&annotation=highlight-p3x69y427)




 "Although one could interpret the phrase, "to engage in other concerted activities," to refer to a situation in which two or more employees are working together at the same time and the same place toward a common goal, the language of § 7 does not confine itself to such a narrow meaning." [Page 3](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/PT2BMCUN?page=3&annotation=highlight-p3x70y323)




 "even the courts that have rejected the Interboro doctrine recognize the possibility that an individual employee may be engaged in concerted activity when he acts alone. They have limited their recognition of this type of concerted activity, however, to two situations: (1) that in which the lone employee intends to induce group activity, and (2) that in which the employee acts as a representative of at least one other employee" [Page 3](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/PT2BMCUN?page=3&annotation=highlight-p3x70y254)




 "The disagreement over the Interboro doctrine, therefore, merely reflects differing views regarding the nature of the relationship that must exist between the action of the individual employee and the actions of the group in order for § 7 to apply." [Page 3](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/PT2BMCUN?page=3&annotation=highlight-p3x70y213)






What's the current status of Board law on this issue? [Page 3](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/PT2BMCUN?page=3&annotation=text-p3x559y224)



 "The invocation of a right rooted in a collective-bargaining agreement is unquestionably an integral part of the process that gave rise to the agreement. That process — beginning with the organization of a union, continuing into the negotiation *832 of a collective-bargaining agreement, and extending through the enforcement of the agreement — is a single, collective activity." [Page 3](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/PT2BMCUN?page=3&annotation=highlight-p3x70y148)




 "when an employee invokes a right grounded in the collective-bargaining agreement, he does not stand alone. Instead, he brings to bear on his employer the power and resolve of all his fellow employees." [Page 3](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/PT2BMCUN?page=3&annotation=highlight-p3x70y94)






What about when there's not union or CBA? [Page 3](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/PT2BMCUN?page=3&annotation=text-p3x549y97)



 "The fact that an activity is concerted, however, does not necessarily mean that an employee can engage in the activity with impunity. An employee may engage in concerted activity in such an abusive manner that he loses the protection of § 7." [Page 5](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/PT2BMCUN?page=5&annotation=highlight-p5x70y422)




 "JUSTICE O'CONNOR, with whom THE CHIEF JUSTICE, JUSTICE POWELL, and JUSTICE REHNQUIST join, dissenting" [Page 7](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/PT2BMCUN?page=7&annotation=highlight-p7x70y591)

And, as *847 several of the Courts of Appeals have concluded, the statutory language can even be stretched to cover an individual who takes action with the proven object of inducing, initiating, or preparing for group action."
